Before you apply

A few minutes of preparation can save you time and help you submit a stronger, more complete application.

  1. [01]Read the role carefullyCheck the duties, requirements, closing date and application instructions before you begin.
  2. [02]Prepare your documentsHave your updated CV, certificates and any other requested documents ready to upload.
  3. [03]Complete all fieldsGive accurate details and fill in every mandatory field before you submit.
  4. [04]Submit before the deadlineApply early so you have time to resolve any technical or document issue.

Need help with your application? Email recruitment@jkuates.jkuat.ac.ke or call 0736 524200, and mention the role you are applying for. JKUATES never charges candidates any fee at any stage of recruitment.
