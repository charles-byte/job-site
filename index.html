FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account to apply?

You can apply directly from any job posting. You may optionally sign in to track your applications and receive job alerts.

Can I apply for more than one position?

Yes. Apply for every role that matches your qualifications and experience.

How will I know the status of my application?

You will receive an email confirmation when you apply. Shortlisted candidates are contacted directly by our recruitment team.

Does JKUATES charge any fees during recruitment?

No. We never ask candidates for money at any stage of recruitment. Please report any such request.
