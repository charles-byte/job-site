  2. [02]Prepare your documentsHave your updated CV, certificates and any other requested documents ready to upload.

Frequently asked questions

Who will employ me if I am successful?

The client organisation you are recruited for. JKUATES manages the recruitment on the client's behalf, from advertising to shortlisting and interviews. Some clients ask us not to name them until a later stage of the process.

Do you charge any fees?

No. JKUATES never charges candidates any fee at any stage of recruitment, including for applying, shortlisting, interviews, medicals or placement. If anyone asks you for money in our name, do not pay, and report it to recruitment@jkuates.jkuat.ac.ke.

How will I know if I have been shortlisted?

Shortlisted candidates are only contacted via recruitment@jkuates.jkuat.ac.ke or iconsult@jkuates.jkuat.ac.ke, using the details in their application. Please keep your contact details up to date.

Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. You may apply for any role you meet the requirements for. Submit a separate application for each one.

Can I apply after the deadline?

No. Each advert closes at the end of its deadline day and is then removed from the site.

What happens to my personal data?

Your application is stored in our recruitment system and used only for recruitment, in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019. The data is shared with the client organisation for the role you applied for, in full compliance with the Act.
